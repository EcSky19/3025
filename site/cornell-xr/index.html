Project · Persistent-memory glasses · 2026

Choosing a face-match threshold with evidence, not a guess

The team's persistent-memory glasses recognize who you're talking to and surface past context. The prototype matched faces with a hardcoded distance threshold. I made it configurable and measured what each value costs.

What I changed

Replaced the hardcoded threshold with a setting, added boundary tests just inside and outside it, and wrote a sweep script reporting error rates and cross-validated operating points.

Why it matters

A wrong match on memory glasses shows the wrong person's history. Picking the threshold from measured error rates makes that tradeoff explicit.
