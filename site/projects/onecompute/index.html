How a job flows

Step through the lifecycle

  1. Step 1

    Opt in

    An employee chooses to share an idle device. Nothing joins the network without consent.

  2. Step 2

    Submit a job

    A workload is sent to the FastAPI orchestrator.

  3. Step 3

    Sign it

    The job is signed, so a device only runs work it can verify.

  4. Step 4

    Run in a sandbox

    The desktop client executes the job sandboxed, isolated from the employee's files.

  5. Step 5

    Watch it live

    The dashboard shows devices, workloads and capacity in real time.
